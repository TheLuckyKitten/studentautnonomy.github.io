Welcome to my Presentation

Autonomy?

I would like to think most of my time spent in school was spent well. I never felt like there was something I missed out on during my time at my current school, or other ones I have attended over the years. However, the more I grow, the more I begin to realize that this sentiment isn't shared by all. I do find my gripes with certain teaching methods, but being in eleventh grade myself I am feeling the curriculum becoming increasingly harder, and at the same time, increasingly difficult to digest. I, just like my classmates I assume, wish for a teaching method that motivates us, while informing us with more density. But Surprisingly, we don't need anything new. We already have a concept that I feel is currently lacking in german schools. And that concept is called:
Student Autonomy.

What is Student Autonomy?

The Magical World of Museums

Amsterdam is a lovely city. I would assume most know it either for its extravagant tulip market, its tasty cheeses, but I would not think you know Amsterdam for its colorful museums. Truth be told, I didn't know of even half of them! But asides from your usual suspects like the Anne-Frank Museum, you wouldn't even Imagine the ammount of different Museums there are there! Multiple Sex Museums, Artist Museums like the Banksy Museum, and even more expected Museums like the Tulip Museum or the Cheese Museum are presented in an unexpected way.

I went to Amsterdam with my class recently, and to finish this Project I visited Three Museums in Total. Granted, doing them all in one day was probably a bad Idea for reasons I will explain later, but when I eventually layed down to collect my thoughts I had a lot of them.

The Lesson on Lessons

So all of this brings upon the question: Why do all of this? Well, I am of the sort of people that usually struggles immensely with language learning. School, unfortunately for some, forces us to learn at least one foreign language. That language being English. But, to the dismay of hard trying teachers, and me, it can be quite difficult to motivate students to actually learn the language. No matter what you do, how interesting the task seems, they just can't be bothered. This might be, because that element of Student Autonomy is missing, or not fully implemented!
